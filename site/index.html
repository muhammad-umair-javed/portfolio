Muhammad Umair Javed

I work on robot manipulation software in ROS 2: a vision-guided pick-and-place pipeline and a waypoint teaching pipeline for a 6-DOF collaborative arm at the Human-Centered Robotics Lab, UET Lahore. I lead my final year project there.

I am a final-year BS Mechatronics and Control Engineering student. I am now studying learning-based manipulation, starting with teleoperation on an SO101 arm.

Selected work

  1. Vision-guided pick and place for test tube sorting

    A ROS 2 pipeline that detects test tubes with a camera and sorts them with a 6-DOF arm, built on MoveIt Task Constructor.

    OngoingManipulation, Perception

  2. Waypoint teaching and straight-line motion

    Record waypoints by hand-guiding the arm, then replay them as straight-line motions through MoveIt.

    OngoingManipulation, Control

  3. UR5 control from MATLAB over ROS 2

    A two-way link between MATLAB and Simulink and a simulated UR5 in Gazebo, with analytical inverse kinematics and joint-space trajectories.

    CompleteControl, Manipulation

  4. CanSat 2D trajectory estimation

    Ground-station software that estimates a CanSat's 2D path from noisy IMU data using a Kalman filter and double integration.

    CompleteEstimation
